Brand system · v4 · typography & pattern

Middle Eastern & Central Asian table — Canggu, Bali

Two voices and one motif carry the whole brand: Cormorant Garamond italic for everything expressive, Oswald for everything functional, and the eight-point square star for everything in between. The cusped cartouche, the scalloped pill and the sunburst dial are retired.

Everything below is drawn by the live site stylesheets. If a specimen changes here, it has changed on the site.

01

Typography

Display — expressive

Cormorant

Headlines, pull quotes, dish names, the one line per section that has to carry feeling.

Always italic, always 400 or 600, never below 24px, never for interface text. Set it large, give it room, and let the measure stay under 640px.

Text — functional

Oswald

Body copy, navigation, labels, prices, captions, buttons. Condensed and quiet — it never competes with the display face.

300 for body, 400 for buttons and hard-working labels, 500 only for uppercase micro-labels under 12px. Cyrillic covered for RU.

Scale contrast · the house move

Fire & rice

Samarkand  ·  Beirut  ·  Tbilisi  ·  Canggu

One display line at 100px or more, answered by tracked caps at 10–11px. Nothing in between on a hero — the middle sizes belong to body copy, not to headlines.

Editorial rhythm

Silk copy runs on a 4px baseline grid: body 16.5/28, small print 13.5/22, labels 12.5/20. A paragraph never exceeds 34 characters per em of measure, and a column never exceeds 640px. Set one drop cap per page at most — the opening paragraph of the longest story — and never inside a card.

02

Pattern · eight-point square star

A square and its 45° twin, single-weight, no fill. Three scales only: 160px structural, 64px field, 34px hairline. Copper on bone, copper soft on teal, silk sand on spice. One structural field per page; everywhere else it is texture under 20% and never behind body copy at full strength.

Do

Bleed the structural field off at least two edges. Let type sit on the pattern, never in a box on top of it. Align the tile to the page gutter so the lattice reads as architecture.

Don’t

No second scale in the same section. No pattern inside a card, button or photo mask. No rotation, no gradient fills, no outline weights other than 0.7 / 1 / 1.6.

03

Buttons — block & inset rule

The scalloped pill is gone. A SILK button is a hard-edged block with a hairline rule set 4px inside its edge — a printed label, not a UI chip. Three ranks: filled for the one action per section, outlined for the alternative, ruled text for everything tertiary. Oswald 400, 12px, 0.2em, uppercase; minimum 56px tall.

05

Logo & lockups

The wordmark is artwork, never set type — no tracking, no italicising, no recolouring beyond the four approved fills. Clear space equals the height of the S on every side. Minimum width 96px for the lockup, 24px for the sun.

07

Retired in v4

Cartouche shapes

silkTag, silkTagTall and silkBadge are removed everywhere. Containers, photo crops and labels are rectangular; the hairline and the pattern do the decorating.

Scalloped pill

silkPill is replaced by the block button with its 4px inset rule. No rounded corners anywhere in the system.

CSS sunburst & letterspaced SILK

Both stand-ins are replaced by the real artwork: the drawn sun and the SILK RESTAURANT lockup.

Interlaced star & jade linework

The eight-point interlaced tile becomes the square star; Jade Night #016C81 stays for focus states and form underlines only.
